# PR ENTERPRISES — Solar Energy, Products & Installation

**PR ENTERPRISES** is a premier solar energy engineering and installation company providing turnkey residential rooftop solar installations, commercial solar power plants, and Tier-1 solar equipment.

This codebase delivers a modern, high-converting, fully responsive business website built with **React**, **Vite**, and **Tailwind CSS**, featuring an interactive 3D solar house simulation and a dedicated **Google & Meta Ads High-Converting Landing Page**.

---

## 🎯 Google & Meta Ads Landing Page (New!)

A purpose-built, distraction-free landing page engineered specifically for paid traffic campaigns (Google Search, Google PMax, Meta Facebook & Instagram Feed / Stories / Reels):

### Key Conversion Optimization Features:
1. **Above-the-Fold Instant Lead Capture Form:**
   - 1-minute submission form capturing Full Name, WhatsApp Number (10 digits), City/Area, Monthly Bill, and System Type.
   - Immediate feedback with celebration confetti and lead confirmation modal.
   - Instant WhatsApp fallback link (`Chat on WhatsApp in 60 seconds`).
2. **Distraction-Free Header:**
   - Zero navigation link leakage — prevents paid visitors from wandering off without converting.
   - Clear Click-to-Call and WhatsApp chat triggers.
3. **Interactive "Before vs After" Electricity Bill Comparison:**
   - Visual slider demonstrating 90% monthly bill reductions and 25-year wealth accumulation.
4. **Transparent Turnkey Package Cards (3 kW, 5 kW, 10 kW):**
   - Direct button clicks pre-select the package in the form and scroll directly to conversion.
5. **Verified Customer Social Proof & Testimonials:**
   - 4.9★ rating with verified customer case studies.
6. **100% Zero-Risk Free Survey Guarantee:**
   - Eliminates customer hesitation by guaranteeing $0 cost if the rooftop isn't feasible.
7. **Mobile Sticky Action Bar:**
   - Always-visible conversion touch targets for Call Now, Claim Subsidy, and WhatsApp.

### How to Access the Ads Landing Page:
You can direct paid ad traffic using any of these URLs:
- `http://localhost:5173/#landing`
- `http://localhost:5173/?page=landing`
- `http://localhost:5173/?source=google_ads`
- `http://localhost:5173/?source=meta_ads`
- Or click the floating **"🎯 Ads Landing Page"** badge in the top right corner of the website.
